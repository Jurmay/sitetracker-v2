# SiteTracker on your phone

Project: **Amochu Section (Phase-I) Monsoon Restoration Works - TRIAL**

## 1. Install (once)
1. Open the SiteTracker link in **Chrome** (Android) or **Safari** (iPhone): `__________________`
2. Sign in with your email and password.
3. Add to home screen:
   - Android: menu ⋮ → **Add to Home screen** (or **Install app**).
   - iPhone: Share button → **Add to Home Screen**.
4. From now on, open SiteTracker from the orange hard-hat icon.

## 2. Pick the project
Tap **Amochu Section (Phase-I) … TRIAL**. The app remembers it next time.

## 3. Every day
- **Master Roll**: mark who worked today. Send before **6 pm**; after that it locks.
- **Progress**: pick the BOQ item (e.g. "BOQ 02: Ch 6.4 km – RRM masonry"), enter the quantity done today, add a photo, Submit.
- A reminder email comes at **12 noon** if nothing has been sent.

## 4. No signal?
Fill in and Submit as normal. The bar at the top says **"No signal… saved on this phone"**.
It is sent by itself when signal returns. Don't enter it twice.
If the bar says **"Not accepted"**, read the reason, fix it, and send again.

## 5. Test today (5 minutes)
1. Send one Master Roll and one Progress report with signal.
2. Turn on **Airplane mode**, send one more Progress report. You should see "saved on this phone".
3. Turn Airplane mode off. Within a minute the bar disappears.
4. Tell the office what was confusing.

This is a trial project. Nothing entered here affects real accounts.
